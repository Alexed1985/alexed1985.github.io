About S&C Building, Joinery & Developments

With over 40 years of combined trade experience, S&C Building, Joinery & Developments delivers top-tier craftsmanship and reliable building services across the Wirral and surrounding areas.

Who We Are

Founded and run by local tradesmen Scott and Carl, our business is built on a reputation for precision, hard work, and complete honesty. Whether transforming a home with a custom staircase or managing a full-scale property extension, we bring the same attention to detail to every job.

We understand that home alterations can feel overwhelming. That’s why we manage projects seamlessly from initial quote to final handover—ensuring minimal disruption to your daily routine.

Our Specialties

  • Kitchen Outfits: Full design, fitting, plumbing, and tiling.
  • Bathroom Renovations: Modern transformations tailored to your space.
  • Home Extensions: Expanding your living space seamlessly.
  • Bespoke Joinery: Custom staircases, fitted storage, and woodwork.
  • General Alterations: Walls removed, structural support, and full refurbishments.
